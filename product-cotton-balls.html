Organic
Cotton Balls

Felice Organic Cotton Balls are designed to provide ultra-soft, gentle care for all skincare, cosmetic, and pediatric applications. Crafted from 100% pure organic cotton fibers, they are highly absorbent, completely sterile, and hypoallergenic.
